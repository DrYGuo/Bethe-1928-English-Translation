# Bethe 1928 English translation - separated edition source package

The combined 118-page edition has been separated into two independent documents.

## Separate documents

1. `Bethe_1928_Final_Audited_Translation_rev20.pdf` - the complete audited translation (91 pages), authoritative for exact equations (1)-(94), tables, figures, footnotes, and documented source-print anomalies.
2. `Bethe_1928_Polished_English_Reading_Edition.pdf` - the polished prose reading edition (26 pages), corresponding to the edited material that formerly occupied the front of the combined edition.
3. `Bethe_1928_Polished_English_Reading_Edition.tex` - standalone LaTeX source for the 26-page polished reading edition. It does not embed the rev20 PDF.

## Supporting audit material

- `Bethe_1928_English_Prose_Review_Report.md`
- `Bethe1928_math_notation_audit_checklist.md`
- `Bethe1928_Audit_Revision_4_Appendix.tex`

The polished reading edition refers to the separately supplied rev20 document whenever exact mathematical/documentary material is required.
