# Working English Translation — September 2026

**Hans Bethe, "Theorie der Beugung von Elektronen an Kristallen," _Annalen der Physik_ 392, 55–129 (1928).**

This repository contains a working scholarly English translation of Bethe's 1928 paper on the theory of electron diffraction by crystals — the founding work of dynamical electron diffraction theory.

## Status

**This is a working translation, not a final published version.**

The translation and mathematical notation have been systematically cross-checked against the original German edition:

- Complete sentence-by-sentence bilingual prose audit (665 units)
- Mathematical notation certified against the German facsimile (numbered equations (1)–(94))
- Verified source-print anomalies preserved as printed and documented

The work has **not yet undergone formal peer review** and may be revised as further errors or ambiguities are identified. See [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) for revision history.
